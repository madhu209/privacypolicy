Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data
While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. This may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address
  • First and last name

Camera and Photo Data
To scan grocery receipts and product barcodes, the Application requests access to Your device's camera. Photos of receipts and barcodes are processed to extract item names, prices, and purchase dates. Captured images may be temporarily stored on Our servers to build and maintain Your pantry inventory, and are retained only as long as needed to provide this feature or as You choose to keep them in Your account.

Pantry and Inventory Data
Information You scan or manually enter — including item names, quantities, purchase dates, and estimated or confirmed expiry dates — is stored so the Application can track Your pantry and send expiry reminders.

Notification Data
To send You reminders before food expires, the Application requests permission to send push notifications. You can disable these at any time in Your device settings.

Usage Data
Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. This may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address, browser type, the pages of Our Service that You visit, the time and date of Your visit, and other diagnostic data.
